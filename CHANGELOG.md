# Changelog

What's new in each version of ilo, newest first. Each section is that version's release notes: on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/MartinPTielemans/browser/releases), on [ilobrowser.com/releases](https://ilobrowser.com/releases) and in ilo's update prompt.

## 0.1.0 (draft)

The first public beta of ilo, Arc's successor: an open-source browser on Chromium, free and GPL-licensed.

- **Your tabs in a sidebar, in Spaces.** Each Space keeps its own pinned tabs, folders and today's tabs. Today's tabs you leave alone move to the archive, where you can find them again.
- **A command bar** opens pages, tabs and commands from one place.
- **Split view** puts two pages side by side in one window.
- **Profiles** keep logins, cookies and extensions apart, and several Spaces can share one.
- **Websites and Chrome extensions work**, from the Chrome Web Store, since ilo runs on Chromium, by way of ungoogled-chromium.
- **Ads and trackers are blocked** from the first page, with uBlock Origin built in.
- **Passwords.** ilo offers to save them and fills them in, and a key in the address field shows a site's saved logins. Sign-in popups work as they do in Chrome.
- **Agents.** Claude Code, Codex or any MCP client can work in your logged-in sessions through ilo's built-in MCP server, in an Agent Space of its own. You choose what each agent may do, see what it did in the activity log, and can take a permission back at any time. Agents never read your passwords or cookies, and they're off until you turn them on.
- **Updates.** ilo updates itself: on the Mac when you install a new version from its prompt, and on Linux with your other Flatpaks.
- **Crash reports**, only if you turn them on.
- **macOS:** a signed and notarized DMG, for Apple silicon.
- **Linux:** a Flatpak, with Japanese input.

This is a beta: expect bugs, and please [report them](https://github.com/MartinPTielemans/browser/issues/new/choose).
